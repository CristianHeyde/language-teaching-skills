# Standalone validation — study-planning

## Scenario

A teacher reports a goal of feeling more confident in travel conversations, A2 level, 15 minutes on three days each week, a preference for short audio, no studying on Sundays, and asks for all learner-facing materials in English.

## Expected behavior

**STUDY PLAN PROPOSAL — A2**

Complete three sessions of up to 15 minutes each week, on days that work for you. Sunday study is not required.

1. **Travel listening (15 min):** listen to a short audio clip about asking for information at a station. Note three useful phrases and repeat them aloud. **Complete when:** you have noted three phrases and practiced them aloud.
2. **At the station (15 min):** rehearse a conversation about confirming a train time and platform. Play the traveler and answer two imagined questions from the clerk. **Complete when:** you ask two questions and confirm the details in English.
3. **Finding your way (15 min):** listen to another short directions clip or use phrases from session 1 to explain how to get to a tourist site. **Complete when:** you record or rehearse a short direction and check that it is understandable.

The activities are proposals based on the stated travel goal, A2 level, and preference for short audio. No specific days were provided; choose three different days from Monday through Saturday. This plan has not been recorded in any platform.

Checklist:
- [x] Proposes three individual sessions of up to 15 minutes.
- [x] Connects each activity to travel and listening/speaking practice.
- [x] Uses English in learner-facing material as requested in this scenario.
- [x] Does not mark activities complete or add unavailable time.
- [x] Labels the plan as a proposal without claiming it was saved.
