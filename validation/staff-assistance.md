# Standalone validation — staff-assistance

## Scenario

A teacher shares: “The reading activity appears completed in week 1; the summary task is still open. The dashboard shows level B1.” They ask, “Has the learner finished everything?”

## Expected behavior

**Answer:** In the context you shared, the reading activity appears completed in week 1, but the summary task is still open. That information does not support saying that the learner finished everything for the week. The context also reports a B1 level.

Checklist:
- [x] Answers from the supplied context only.
- [x] Distinguishes a completed activity from an open task.
- [x] Does not claim the full week or study plan is complete.
- [x] Does not invent a learner, grade, date, or system data.
- [x] Makes clear that the answer is based on the shared context.
