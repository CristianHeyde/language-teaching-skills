# Standalone validation — task-design

## Scenario

A teacher asks for a task for a B1 learner to practice asking for clarification in an online meeting. There are 20 minutes, the interaction is in pairs, and materials should be in English.

## Expected behavior

**Framing for the teacher:** **PROPOSAL** for practicing clarification requests in an online meeting. It uses the stated B1 level, pair format, and 20-minute duration.

### Clarification in a Meeting

**Goal:** Ask for clarification when you do not understand a point in an online meeting.

**Situation:** You and a partner are planning a small event. One person shares a schedule with one unclear detail. Ask questions until you understand the time, place, or next step.

**Instructions**
1. Student A: Choose one detail from the schedule and explain it briefly. Keep one important detail unclear.
2. Student B: Listen and ask at least two clarification questions. Try phrases such as “Could you say that again?”, “What do you mean by…?”, and “Do you mean…?”
3. Student A: Clarify the detail. Student B: Restate the information to check understanding.
4. Switch roles and use a different detail.

**Time:** 20 minutes. **Interaction:** pairs. **Product:** two short meeting exchanges, with each learner asking for clarification.

**Success check:** Each learner asks two relevant clarification questions and correctly restates one clarified detail.

Checklist:
